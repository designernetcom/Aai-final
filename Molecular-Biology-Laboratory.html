Molecular Biology Laboratory

Aai Women’s Hospital, our cutting-edge Molecular Biology Laboratory stands at the forefront of modern diagnostics, offering advanced molecular and genetic testing that is essential for accurate diagnosis, early detection, and personalized treatment planning. This highly specialized laboratory supports the hospital’s commitment to precision medicine and plays a pivotal role in managing complex medical conditions. The Molecular Biology Lab is equipped with state-of-the-art instrumentation and follows internationally recognized protocols to perform sophisticated diagnostic techniques, including: Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) for detecting viruses, bacteria, and genetic mutations Real-Time PCR (qPCR) for quantifying viral load and gene expression DNA and RNA extraction and sequencing Mutation analysis and genetic profiling Gene expression and epigenetic testing Carrier screening and prenatal genetic testing Oncology panels for identifying tumor markers and actionable mutations

These technologies enable the detection of inherited genetic disorders, chromosomal abnormalities, rare diseases, infectious pathogens (like TB, HPV, Hepatitis, and COVID-19), and cancer-associated mutations, often before clinical symptoms appear. By identifying the root molecular causes of a disease, the lab empowers clinicians to offer individualized, targeted therapies and guide patients toward better treatment outcomes.
